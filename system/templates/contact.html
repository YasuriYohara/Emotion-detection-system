Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the emotion detection?

Our system achieves high accuracy rates using state-of-the-art deep learning models trained on extensive datasets. Results may vary based on image quality and facial visibility.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. All images are processed temporarily and immediately deleted after analysis. We do not store, transmit, or share any facial data with third parties.

What image formats are supported?

We currently support JPEG and PNG formats with a maximum file size of 10MB. For best results, use clear, well-lit frontal face photographs.
