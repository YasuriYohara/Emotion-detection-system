How It Works

  1. Upload a facial photograph (JPEG or PNG format)
  2. The system detects the face using computer vision
  3. A deep learning model analyzes the facial expression
  4. The predicted emotion and confidence score are displayed
  5. Your image is processed temporarily and immediately deleted
