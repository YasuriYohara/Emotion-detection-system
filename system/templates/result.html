Understanding Your Results

Confidence Score

Indicates how certain the model is about its prediction. Scores above 60% are generally reliable, while scores below 40% suggest the expression may be ambiguous or mixed.

Emotion Scores

Shows the probability distribution across all seven emotion categories. The highest score determines the predicted emotion. Secondary emotions may indicate mixed or transitional expressions.

Best Practices

For accurate results, use clear, well-lit photos with frontal face orientation. Avoid sunglasses, masks, or heavy shadows. The system works best with neutral backgrounds and single-person images.

Technical Notes

  • Model: FER (Facial Expression Recognition) trained on FER2013 dataset
  • Detection Method: Haar Cascade with MTCNN fallback
  • Emotion Categories: 7 universal facial expressions (Ekman & Friesen, 1978)
  • Processing: Local, temporary, no data retention
